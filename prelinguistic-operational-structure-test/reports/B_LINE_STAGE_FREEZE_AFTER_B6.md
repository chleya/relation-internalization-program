# B-Line Stage Freeze After B6

## Purpose

The B-line has become a strong diagnostic and discriminator stack for toy operational structure.

It progressed from closed-loop inspection and intervention to risk-constrained actionability, delayed credit, trace repair diagnostics, mechanism necessity, transfer remapping, hard-remap pressure, and combined-remap failure attribution.

## What B-Line Achieved

The B-line now tests whether hand-specified or hand-scaffolded operational components remain useful under:

* missing masks
* wrong trace
* hidden public-state cues
* delayed outcomes
* candidate-search pressure
* hard mask visibility
* transfer remaps
* mechanism ablations

It provides useful falsification pressure. It can say when a proposed operational structure is shortcut-explainable, leakage-prone, or too brittle.

## Why It Is Now a Discriminator

B-line diagnostics assume many structural components already exist:

* trace state
* selector behavior
* update rules
* feedback history
* actionability masks
* delayed credit buffers
* candidate search

The recent B6.x work mostly asks whether these components are necessary, robust, transferable, or shortcut-explainable. That is valuable, but it remains discriminator-first.

## Diminishing Returns

Further B-line refinements can continue to sharpen failure attribution, but they increasingly test hand-designed operational scaffolds.

The next scientific bottleneck is not another diagnostic split. The next bottleneck is whether some part of the operational structure can be generated from pressure.

## Move to G-Line

G-line starts the generator side of the project.

The question changes from:

> Does a proposed operational structure survive diagnostic pressure?

to:

> Can operational structure be generated from prediction error, compression, intervention feedback, action usefulness, and OOD pressure?

## Claim Boundary

Freezing the B-line as a strong diagnostic ladder does not prove language-free cognition, real-world risk intelligence, robotics capability, safety certification, construction-site autonomy, or deployable engineering control.
