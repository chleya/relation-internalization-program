# Overclaim Checklist

## Forbidden Claims

- The agents understand relations.
- The system solves relation reasoning.
- The neural models have general causal understanding.
- Edit-pressure training solves neural relation internalization.
- The result applies to large language models.
- The result replaces LLMs.
- The result proves object permanence.
- The slope toy is real slope monitoring.
- The system is deployment-ready engineering AI.
- The agents provide real geotechnical safety capability.
- The framework performs general causal discovery.
- The inspection policy is calibrated for real sensors.
- The system is robust to adversarial missingness beyond tested cases.
- Editable behavior proves relation internalization.

## Preferred Safe Wording

Use:

```text
passes diagnostic gates
```

Instead of:

```text
understands relations
```

Use:

```text
relation-internalization-like behavior in a toy setting
```

Instead of:

```text
neural relation understanding
```

Use:

```text
counterfactual training is the strongest current non-handwritten positive condition
```

Instead of:

```text
neural models naturally internalize relations
```

Use:

```text
edit-pressure training is mixed and exposes an editability false positive
```

Instead of:

```text
edit-pressure succeeds
```

Use:

```text
toy engineering-style relation chain
```

Instead of:

```text
engineering safety model
```

Use:

```text
rule-based inspection value in a toy diagnostic
```

Instead of:

```text
calibrated inspection policy
```

## Required Distinctions

Clearly distinguish:

1. table-level editability;
2. edit-state responsiveness;
3. support-conditioned relation binding;
4. stable causal relation subspace.

Do not collapse these into a single claim of relation internalization.
